# Nutritail partner launch pack

Status: operational baseline for a pilot, pending legal and tax review

Version: 2026-08-17

This folder contains the material needed to onboard the first local store or
online pet shop without changing nutrition recommendations.

- `partner-agreement-draft-el.md`: Greek commercial agreement draft.
- `onboarding-runbook-el.md`: internal intake, verification, launch and
  offboarding checklist.

Before sending the agreement to a business, replace every bracketed field and
have Greek counsel confirm the final legal entity, tax, consumer, P2B,
competition, liability, governing-law and dispute terms.

The product rules remain non-negotiable:

1. A partner never changes food suitability or nutrition scoring.
2. A listing must match the exact Food V2 product already selected.
3. Paid ranking is possible only among eligible listings for that product.
4. Every paid placement is visibly labelled.
5. Partners receive aggregate referral metrics only, never customer or pet data.
